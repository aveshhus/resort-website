Situated along Sikar Road opposite the power house in Udaipurwati, Nangal, Shrii Palace Resort is purposefully engineered to host magnificent destination weddings, multi-day family milestones, and grand cultural gatherings in Shekhawati.

From dramatic Baraat processions through ceremonial arrival gates to starlit evening receptions across expansive manicured lawns, every moment is supported by generous event spaces and authentic Rajasthani warmth.

Spaces of Distinction

Celebration Venues

A versatile spectrum of indoor air-conditioned ballrooms, grand outdoor wedding grounds, and intimate celebration courtyards.

Venue 01 • Open Air

The Grand Celebration Lawn

An expansive 30,000+ sq.ft. carpet of manicured grass flanked by majestic date palms and expansive skies. Engineered for grand Sangeet nights, Varmala ceremonies, starlit dining, and fireworks for over 1,500 guests.

Venue 02 • Climate Controlled

Imperial Pillarless AC Ballroom

A high-ceiling indoor ballroom with crystal chandeliers, acoustic ceiling panels, and dedicated stage dais. Offers an all-weather climate-controlled sanctuary for formal banquets, ring ceremonies, and musical performances.

Venue 03 • Ceremonial Stage

Ceremonial Mandap & Stage Dais

Elevated wedding stage crafted specifically for royal Mandap rituals, sacred pheras, and bridal entries. Offers pristine unobstructed sightlines from all corners of the grounds for memorable photography.

Venue 04 • Courtyard Terrace

Pavilion & Courtyard Terraces

Sheltered architectural verandas, jaali screens, and paved event terraces designed for joyful Mehendi afternoons, vibrant Haldi rituals, and afternoon high-tea gatherings surrounded by fresh greenery.

Guest Comfort & Event Stays

Accommodations & Host Suites

Designed specifically for multi-day wedding parties, immediate families, and bridal preparation with dedicated air-conditioned comfort.

Hospitality Desk

Royal Reception Lounge

Opulent air-conditioned arrival lobby featuring royal sofa seating, crystal chandeliers, and dedicated concierge desk for seamless guest check-ins and key distribution.

Host Suite

Royal Family Host Suite

Spacious deluxe room tailored for bride and groom families, complete with plush king bedding, climate control, generous wardrobe space, and peaceful views.

Bridal Wing

Bridal Dressing Suite Entrance

Distinct private suite wing accessed through an ornate handcrafted wooden Rajasthani doorway, providing quiet sanctuary for bridal hair, makeup, and attire fittings.

Leisure & Feasting

Poolside Leisure & Dining Facilities

From afternoon relaxation by the turquoise water to opulent multi-course wedding feasts and live culinary counters.

Azure Swimming Pool

Sparkling open-air pool mirroring the ochre hotel towers, framed by sun loungers and mountain breezes. Perfect for daytime cooldowns and poolside high-tea.

Garden View Restaurant

Comfortable air-conditioned dining hall serving pure vegetarian culinary delights, traditional Rajasthani thalis, and multi-cuisine fare for residential guests.

Grand Banquet Buffets

Professional culinary presentation with live interactive cooking stations, authentic Shekhawati sweets, and expansive buffet counters serving 1,500+ guests.

Illuminated Pool Deck

Magical evening illumination casting warm golden reflections across the water, providing an enchanting backdrop for cocktail hours and musical evenings.
