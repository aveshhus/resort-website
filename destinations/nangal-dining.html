Pure Farm-To-Table Heritage

From In-House Gaushala to Your Plate

At Shrii Palace Nangal, our hospitality begins with the sacred cow. We maintain our own private Gaushala on the resort estate, rearing pure indigenous Gir and Desi cows on organic fodder.

Every morning, our master chefs receive fresh, warm A2 milk. From traditional slow-simmered kheer and hand-churned butter to authentic Daal Baati Churma cooked in pure bilona ghee, our food is nourishing, authentic, and unforgettable.
