The Perfect Road Trip from Jaipur & Delhi

Shrii Palace Nangal is nestled against the majestic Aravalli hills, offering scenic mountain views directly from Sikar Road.

With broad paved access roads, secure on-site parking, and proximity to major regional landmarks such as Shakambhari Mata Temple (14 km) and Kot Dam, it serves as a serene weekend retreat.
