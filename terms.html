Terms & Resort Policies

Guidelines for room reservations, destination wedding bookings, check-in, and property care.

1. Check-In & Check-Out Times

Standard resort check-in time is 14:00 hrs (2:00 PM) and check-out time is 11:00 hrs (11:00 AM). Early check-in or late check-out is subject to availability and prior confirmation with the front desk.

2. Guest Identification

As per Indian government regulations, all adult guests must present a valid government-issued photo identity proof (Aadhaar Card, Passport, Voter ID, or Driving License) upon arrival.

3. Wedding & Event Bookings

Wedding lawns, banquet halls, and bulk room blocks are secured upon receipt of the agreed advance deposit. Detailed event timelines, sound decibel limits, and fire safety guidelines must be adhered to in accordance with local administration laws.

4. Cancellation & Amendment Policy

Individual room reservation cancellation requests made at least 7 days prior to arrival are eligible for full refund or date alteration. Group and wedding booking deposits are governed by the specific terms in the signed hospitality contract.

5. Central Contact Desk

For reservation assistance and billing queries, reach our central office at +91 95097 71500 or book@nangalresort.com.
