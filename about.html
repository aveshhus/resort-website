Our Story & Heritage

Rooted in Rajasthan.
Crafted for Moments.

Shrii Palace Resorts was created to offer two distinctive expressions of luxury hospitality in Shekhawati — uniting grand celebrations with mountain tranquility.

Hospitality Philosophy

The soul of
generous hospitality.

In Rajasthan, hospitality is an art form passed down through centuries. At Shrii Palace Resorts, this heritage is expressed through expansive open lawns, traditional architectural motifs, handcrafted terracotta murals, and warm personal attention.

Whether orchestrating a multi-day wedding celebration at Shrii Palace Resort or providing an unhurried mountain escape at Shrii Palace Marriage Garden with pure farm-fresh A2 cow dairy, our single brand standard remains steadfast: thoughtful, gracious, and authentic.
