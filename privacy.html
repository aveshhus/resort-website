Privacy Policy

How we collect, protect, and respect guest information across Shrii Palace Resorts.

1. Information We Collect

When you enquire or reserve a stay, wedding, or banquet with Shrii Palace Resorts, we may collect your name, phone number, email address, dates of travel, and event requirements strictly to facilitate your hospitality experience.

2. How Your Information is Used

Your details are used exclusively by our in-house reservations and concierge teams to respond to enquiries, provide quotations, coordinate event logistics, and ensure seamless guest check-in at Udaipurwati and Nangal properties.

3. Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade guest personal data to any external marketing agencies. Data is shared only with verified payment gateways or legal authorities if required by Indian law.

4. Contact for Privacy Inquiries

For any questions regarding your personal information, please email our privacy desk at book@nangalresort.com or call +91 95097 71500.
